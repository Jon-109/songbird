Drop your meeting photos in this folder (JPG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF).
They will automatically appear in the "Community Photos" carousel on the homepage.

Tips:
- Name files in the order you want them shown, e.g. 01.jpg, 02.jpg ... 13.jpg
- Landscape (wide) photos look best; they're displayed at a 4:3 crop.
- After adding photos in dev mode, just refresh the page. For the live site,
  the site needs to be rebuilt/redeployed for new photos to appear.
